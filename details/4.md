# Trending Content Discovery

## Description

- Surfaces popular content aligned with current trends in the world to enhance user engagement.
- Highlights content that is currently popular among the broader audience.

## Challenges

- Requires real-time data processing to continuously update trending content lists.
- Trending content may not match individual user preference.

## Algorithm: Popularity-Based Filtering

Popularity-Based Filtering recommends items based on their popularity among all users or a specific segment of users. It prioritizes items with maximum interaction rates and views.
It also identifies and recommends content that is currently popular and focuses on maximizing user engagement with widely accepted content.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** O(n), [n is the number of items].
- **Space Complexity:** O(1), typically requires minimal additional space beyond the item popularity scores.
