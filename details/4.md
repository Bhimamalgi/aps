# Trending Content Discovery

## Market Benefits
- Quickly surfaces popular content aligned with current trends, enhancing user engagement.
- Improves user satisfaction by highlighting content that is currently popular among a broader audience.

## Challenges
- Requires real-time data processing capabilities to continuously update trending content.
- Trending content may not always match individual user tastes and preferences.

## Algorithm: Popularity-Based Filtering
Popularity-Based Filtering recommends items based on their popularity among all users or a specific segment of users. It prioritizes items with high interaction rates or views.

### Algorithm Explanation
Popularity-Based Filtering identifies and recommends content that is currently popular across the platform or specific user segments. It focuses on maximizing user engagement by suggesting widely accepted content.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** O(n), where n is the number of items.
- **Space Complexity:** O(1), as it typically requires minimal additional space beyond the item popularity scores.
