# Trending Content 

## Description

- Promotes content that is currently popular among a wider audience.
- Surfaces popular content that is in line with global trends to increase user interaction.

## Challenges

- Requires real-time data in order to update trending media continuously.
- Trending content may not be to user's preference.

## Algorithm: Popularity-Based Filtering

Depending on their level of popularity, suggests films and television series. Sorting content according to its popularity at the moment, giving priority to things with the highest interaction rates and views, and emphasising user engagement with commonly accepted content.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** O(n), [n is the number of items].
- **Space Complexity:** O(1), typically requires minimal additional space beyond the item popularity scores.
