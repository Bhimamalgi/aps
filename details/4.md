# Trending Content 

## Description

- Surfaces popular content aligned with current trends in the world to enhance user engagement.
- Promoting content that is currently popular among wider audience.

## Challenges

- Needs real-time data to continuously update trending media.
- Trending content may not match individual user preference.

## Algorithm: Popularity-Based Filtering

Popularity-Based Filtering recommends items based on their popularity among all users or a specific segment of users.Prioritizing items with maximum interaction rates and views and identifying content that is currently popular and focusing on maximizing user engagement with widely accepted content.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** O(n), [n is the number of items].
- **Space Complexity:** O(1), typically requires minimal additional space beyond the item popularity scores.
