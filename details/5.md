# Seasonal Content Suggestion

## Market Benefits
- Enhances user experience by recommending content relevant to seasonal events or holidays.
- Increases user engagement during specific times of the year with thematic content suggestions.

## Challenges
- Requires timely updates and adjustments to content recommendations based on seasonal changes.
- May struggle with accurately predicting user preferences for seasonal content.

## Algorithm: Time-Based Filtering
Time-Based Filtering recommends items based on temporal factors, such as release date or seasonal relevance. It adjusts recommendations dynamically to reflect current or upcoming events.

### Algorithm Explanation
Time-Based Filtering tailors recommendations based on time-sensitive factors, ensuring users receive relevant content aligned with seasonal events or trends. It updates recommendations dynamically as seasonal preferences change.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** O(n), where n is the number of items considered within the time window.
- **Space Complexity:** O(n), where n is the number of items.
