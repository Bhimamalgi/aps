# Diverse Content Exploration

## Description

- Encourage users to explore a wide range of content, enhancing curiosity and satisfaction.
- Increases user engagement as it offers diverse recommendations which deliver various and new interests.

## Challenges

- Balancing relevance with diversity is challenging.
- Ensuring proper feedback on diverse recommendations with user preference.

## Algorithm: Diversity-Aware Recommender Systems

Diversity-Aware Recommender Systems tries to provide recommendations that balance between popular and niche content, promoting diversity in user selections while maintaining relevance.
Improves recommendation diversity by considering the popularity and uniqueness of content. They adjust recommendations for varied user preferences, to deliver a balanced combo of popular and niche content.

### Complexity

- **Time Complexity:** O(n * m), [n is the number of items and m is the diversity measure complexity].
- **Space Complexity:** O(n), [n is the number of items].
